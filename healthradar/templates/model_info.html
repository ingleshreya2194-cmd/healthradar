How it works

  • Risk classifier: a Random Forest looks at cases in the last 4 weeks, the growth rate over the last 2 weeks, population and cases per 100,000 people, then labels a region Low, Medium or High.
  • Forecast: predicts weekly cases for the next 4 weeks from past weekly cases. The shaded band on the charts shows a likely range, which gets wider further into the future.
  • Limits: these are statistical estimates for awareness. They can be wrong and are not medical advice or an official health alert.
